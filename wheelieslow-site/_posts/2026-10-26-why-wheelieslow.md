I started riding bikes at 19. I'm 39 now, and for most of the twenty years in between, riding was the thing that organized my life.

Through my twenties I raced a lot. I worked my way up to Cat 1 on the mountain bike and Cat 2 in cyclocross, and the further a race got from pavement, the more I liked it. In the winter I skied, and when I needed something to keep me honest between seasons, I picked up cross-country skiing too.

## The last six years

Then my body started sending bills.

In 2020 I tore my ACL skiing. That meant surgery, a long rehab, and a slow return to the bike. I got there. In 2024 I was diagnosed with costochondritis and spent the better part of a year off the bike. In January 2026 I hurt my SI joint playing pickleball, of all things.

Years ago someone told me my lack of stretching would catch up with me. It did.

## What's changed

I'm spending real time in the gym now, lifting, and I've started climbing. Neither of those was ever part of the plan when I was racing. Both feel like the reason I might still be racing at 40.

## What this site is for

Wheelieslow is where I'm going to document the comeback:

- **The 2027 race season.** Gravel in the spring and, if the lotteries go my way, the Leadville series in the summer. The [season page](/season/) has the full calendar.
- **Race reports and training notes.** What worked, what didn't, and what the numbers say.
- **Life off the bike.** Ski days, climbing and the trips in between.

The short clips go on Instagram, the race films go on YouTube, and the full story lives here. Thanks for following along.
