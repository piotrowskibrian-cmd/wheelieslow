About

Hi, I'm Brian.

I've been riding bikes for twenty years, and these days I'm working my way back to riding them well.

{% if site.about_photo != "" %} {% else %} {% include logo-wheel.html size="200" %} {% endif %}
{% capture story %} I started riding at 19. Through my twenties I raced a lot, and raced pretty well: I made it to **Cat 1 on the mountain bike** and **Cat 2 in cyclocross**. Off-road was always where I was happiest. Winters were for skiing. When I needed something to keep the motivation up between race seasons, I picked up cross-country skiing too. Wheelieslow came out of those years on the bike, and the name stuck. ## Then the injuries started My mid-to-late thirties have been a run of setbacks: - **2020:** tore my ACL in a skiing accident. Surgery, rehab, and eventually back on the bike. - **2024:** diagnosed with costochondritis and off the bike for almost a year. - **January 2026:** hurt my SI joint playing pickleball. Someone once told me my lack of stretching would catch up with me. They were right. {% include photo.html src="/assets/img/about-race.jpg" alt="Brian racing cyclocross in a red and black kit" %} ## So this is the comeback I'm 39 now, and I'm spending more time in the gym lifting and on the wall climbing. I'm trying to build a body that can keep doing all of this into my forties. The 2027 season is the test: gravel in the spring, the Leadville races in the summer, and an honest account of what it takes to get there. This site is where the full story lives: [race reports and training notes]({{ '/journal/' | relative_url }}), [the 2027 calendar]({{ '/season/' | relative_url }}), and the occasional ski or climbing trip. {% endcapture %} {{ story | markdownify }}
